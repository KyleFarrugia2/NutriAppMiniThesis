IEEE conference paper (IEEEtran)
================================

Prerequisites: MiKTeX or TeX Live with IEEEtran class and IEEEtran.bst.

From this folder (paper/):

  pdflatex nutri_work_ieee.tex
  bibtex nutri_work_ieee
  pdflatex nutri_work_ieee.tex
  pdflatex nutri_work_ieee.tex

Output: nutri_work_ieee.pdf

Replace author block in nutri_work_ieee.tex with your name, department, and email before submission.

If bibtex fails on first run, ensure references.bib is in the same directory as the .tex file.
