Viva Beamer deck
================

Build (from viva/):

  pdflatex viva_presentation.tex
  pdflatex viva_presentation.tex

Optional: latexmk -pdf viva_presentation.tex

Viva constraints (per brief):
- Maximum about 10 minutes spoken content.
- Embed or show a screen recording of the working prototype (trim to at most 2 minutes). Easiest: record separately, insert video in PDF only if your Beamer setup supports multimedia; otherwise play the clip alongside the slides during the live viva.

Replace Your Name / Your University on the title slide.
